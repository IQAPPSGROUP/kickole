# Kickolé — internal tester instructions

Use these notes for **TestFlight** and **Google Play internal testing**. Public App Store / Play production must stay unpublished until the remaining blockers in [`RELEASE_VALIDATION.md`](./RELEASE_VALIDATION.md) are cleared.

## What to check

- **Choose your kit** on the menu, in Settings, and in Customise. Neutral is the default. The choice stays on the device.
- There is no shop, no Restore Purchases, and no in-app purchase. Do not create store products for this build.

## Device checklist (testers)

- Small and larger phones; portrait safe areas; Android system Back from Customise/Settings.
- Kit list scrolls; the player preview updates; background/resume mid-run; Low/Medium/High graphics.
- Hair follows the head during kicks, stalls and freestyle tricks.
- Environments and movement still feel correct. The menu opens on the gameplay camera. There is no opening flyby.
