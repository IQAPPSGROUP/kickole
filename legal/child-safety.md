# KICKOLÉ child safety statement

Version 1.0. Last updated 25 October 2026.

KICKOLÉ is a football game that children play. This statement explains how it is
designed to keep them safe. It sits alongside our privacy notice and terms of use.

## Designed for children by default

- **No accounts.** There is nothing to sign up to and no profile to fill in.
- **No personal details are ever asked for** — no name, email, age or date of birth.
- **No chat and no messaging.** Players cannot contact each other.
- **No in-app purchases** and no coin balances, so there is nothing to spend
  money on inside the game.
- **No advertising**, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
- **No location access.** We never ask for GPS or device location.
- **No profiling, streaks or behavioural tracking.**

## The leaderboard

The leaderboard is the only shared feature and it is **off until the player taps
Join leaderboard**.

- A player's display name is a **nickname picked from our own word lists**, not
  anything they type freely.
- Those lists are **screened for offensive, abusive and identifying words**,
  including blocked word pairs and name-like combinations.
- A nickname can be changed only **once every 7 days**, which limits use of the
  board as a messaging channel.
- **Report** is available on leaderboard entries, with clear reason codes.
- A report is kept for up to 90 days and is not disclosed to the reported player.
- Players can leave at any time with **Delete my leaderboard data**.

## Safeguarding position

- We do not allow content that sexualises, grooms, bullies or harasses anyone.
- We do not allow sharing of personal contact details, and the nickname system is
  designed to make that difficult.
- Where we become aware of a safeguarding concern involving a child, we will act on
  it and will involve the police or the relevant authority where the law or the
  seriousness of the concern requires it.
- Parents and carers can contact us at hello@iqappsgroup.co.uk about any concern,
  including a nickname or a leaderboard entry.

## What parents and carers should know

- The game can be played entirely offline and without sharing anything.
- If your child joins the leaderboard, the only things shared are a nickname from
  our lists, a score, a chosen country and a random device id created on the phone.
- There is no way for another player to message your child through the game.
- You can remove your child's leaderboard entry at any time from Settings.

## Not a social network

KICKOLÉ is a single-player game with an optional scoreboard. It is not a social
network, it has no user-to-user messaging, and it is not an emergency or support
service. If a child is in immediate danger, call **999**. For non-urgent
safeguarding advice in the UK, the **NSPCC helpline** is 0808 800 5000.
