## The short version

KICKOLÉ has **no accounts and no sign-in**. You can play the whole game without
giving us anything. The only information that ever leaves your device is the
leaderboard entry you choose to send, and only if you tap **Join leaderboard**.

## The leaderboard

The leaderboard is **off until you tap Join leaderboard**. If you join, we send:

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A **nickname** you pick from our word lists | So other players can see who set the score |
| A **random device id** created on your device | To keep your scores together and let you delete them |
| Your **score** | The leaderboard itself |
| A **country** you choose, or International | Country boards. We never use GPS or a network lookup to work out where you are |
| A **hashed IP address**, kept for up to **30 days** | To limit cheating. We store only a hash, not the raw address |

Choosing **International** keeps you off every country board.

**Reports.** If someone reports your nickname or score, we keep that report for up
to **90 days** so we can look at it. We do not tell you who reported you.

**Deleting your leaderboard data.** Tap **Delete my leaderboard data** in Settings.
That removes your nickname and scores. Reports that are still open are kept for
their 90 days, then deleted.

## What we do not do

- No account, no email address, no name, no date of birth.
- **No in-app purchases** and no coin balances. The game is bought once, upfront.
- **No advertising**, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
- **No chat.** Players cannot message each other.
- **No location.** We never ask for GPS or device location.
- We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

## Children

KICKOLÉ is a cartoon football game that children play, so we have followed the
**ICO's Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code)**:

- no accounts and no personal details asked for;
- the leaderboard is off until you choose to join;
- nicknames are chosen from our own word lists, which are screened for offensive,
  abusive and identifying words, and you can change yours only once every 7 days;
- no chat, no advertising, no profiling and no behavioural tracking;
- no nudge techniques to push you into sharing more;
- a **Report** option on every leaderboard entry, with clear reasons.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or carer before you join the leaderboard.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of
your data, and to data portability. For leaderboard data, the quickest route is
**Delete my leaderboard data** in Settings. For anything else, email
privacy@iqappsgroup.co.uk. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we use your data, please contact us first. You can
also complain to the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** at ico.org.uk or
on 0303 123 1113.
