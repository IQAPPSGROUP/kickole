Kickolé music, crowd and cheers
--------------------------------
Menu and gameplay music are the four instrumental songs in
public/audio/rob/ when those files are present. The game tries the
.m4a first and falls back to the .ogg. If both fail to load, the
generated boom-bap bed in src/game/musicBed.ts plays instead.

The four songs: the four instrumental songs in public/audio/rob/ were
created for Kickolé in ElevenLabs, on a paid plan (Creator) under an
account in Rob's name, in October 2026. They are used in Kickolé,
including the paid commercial build, under that licence.

Kicks, misses, stalls, the crowd murmur and the milestone cheers
are synthesised in src/game/audio.ts (Web Audio oscillators and
noise). No third-party sample library is included.

The synthesis is original to this repository. IQ Apps Group may use it
in Kickolé, including a paid commercial build. It is not derived from
Kenney, OpenGameArt, Pixabay or any other sound pack.

The fallback bed is an original boom-bap synthesis: kick and sub,
snare and clap, swung hi-hats, an 808-style bass, filtered chord
voices and a vinyl crackle. Gameplay is the street loop at 92 BPM.
The menu is a sparser variation at 90 BPM. No recorded drum breaks
or sample library are used for that fallback.

The four songs are the files in public/audio/rob/ when they are present.
The fallback bed and the sound effects are synthesised here and need
no further attribution.
