Kickolé music, crowd and cheers
--------------------------------
Everything you hear is synthesised in the game by src/game/audio.ts
and src/game/musicBed.ts (Web Audio oscillators and noise). No
third-party recordings, sample libraries or music tracks are included.

The synthesis is original to this repository. IQ Apps Group may use it
in Kickolé, including a paid commercial build. It is not derived from
Kenney, OpenGameArt, Pixabay or any other sound pack.

Kicks, misses, stalls, the crowd murmur and the milestone cheers are
generated in audio.ts. The background music is an original boom-bap
synthesis in musicBed.ts: kick and sub, snare and clap, swung hi-hats,
an 808-style bass, filtered chord voices and a vinyl crackle. Gameplay
is the street loop at 92 BPM. The menu is a sparser variation at 90 BPM.
No recorded drum breaks, sample library or published composition is used.

There is nothing to decode, stream or attribute beyond this note.
