Kickolé music, crowd and cheers
--------------------------------
Everything you hear is synthesised in the game by src/game/audio.ts
(Web Audio oscillators and noise). No third-party recordings, sample
libraries or music tracks are included.

The synthesis is original to this repository. IQ Apps Group may use it
in Kickolé, including a paid commercial build. It is not derived from
Kenney, OpenGameArt, Pixabay or any other sound pack.

Kicks, misses, stalls, the crowd murmur and the milestone cheers are
generated by that file. The background music is an original steel-pan
synthesis in the same file: a tenor melody, off-beat double notes and
a bass pan. Gameplay is the upbeat street-party loop. The menu is the
same progression, slower and with fewer notes. No recorded steel band,
sample library or published composition is used.

There is nothing to decode, stream or attribute beyond this note.
