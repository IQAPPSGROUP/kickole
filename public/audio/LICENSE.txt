Kickolé music, crowd and cheers
--------------------------------
Everything you hear is synthesised in the game by src/game/audio.ts
(Web Audio oscillators and noise). No third-party recordings, sample
libraries or music tracks are included.

The synthesis is original to this repository. IQ Apps Group may use it
in Kickolé, including a paid commercial build. It is not derived from
Kenney, OpenGameArt, Pixabay or any other sound pack.

Kicks, misses, stalls, the menu bed, the street-football gameplay bed,
the crowd murmur and the milestone cheers are all generated by that
file. There is nothing to decode, stream or attribute beyond this note.
