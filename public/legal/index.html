The short version

KICKOLÉ has no accounts and no sign-in. You can play the whole game without giving us anything. The only information that ever leaves your device is the leaderboard entry you choose to send, and only if you tap Join leaderboard.

The leaderboard

The leaderboard is off until you tap Join leaderboard. If you join, we send:

WhatWhy
A nickname you pick from our word listsSo other players can see who set the score
A random device id created on your deviceTo keep your scores together and let you delete them
Your scoreThe leaderboard itself
A country you choose, or InternationalCountry boards, and a flag beside your nickname. We never use GPS or a network lookup to work out where you are
A hashed IP address, kept for up to 30 daysTo limit cheating. We store only a hash, not the raw address

Choosing International keeps you off every country board and shows no flag beside your nickname.

Reports. If someone reports your nickname or score, we keep that report for up to 90 days so we can look at it. We do not tell you who reported you.

Deleting your leaderboard data. Tap Delete my leaderboard data in Settings. That removes your nickname and scores. Reports that are still open are kept for their 90 days, then deleted.

What we do not do

  • No account, no email address, no name, no date of birth.
  • No in-app purchases and no coin balances. The game is bought once, upfront.
  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No chat. Players cannot message each other.
  • No location. We never ask for GPS or device location.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

Children

KICKOLÉ is a cartoon football game that children play, so we have followed the ICO's Children's Code (Age Appropriate Design Code):

  • no accounts and no personal details asked for;
  • the leaderboard is off until you choose to join;
  • nicknames are chosen from our own word lists, which are screened for offensive,

abusive and identifying words, and you can change yours only once every 7 days;

  • no chat, no advertising, no profiling and no behavioural tracking;
  • no nudge techniques to push you into sharing more;
  • a Report option on every leaderboard entry, with clear reasons.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or carer before you join the leaderboard.

Who helps us

  • Supabase — stores the leaderboard entries. Data is held in London, UK.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the game.
  • Apple and Google — handle the purchase, as separate controllers. We never

see your payment details.

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or safeguards UK law accepts, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

KICKOLÉ child safety statement

Version 1.0. Last updated 25 October 2026.

KICKOLÉ is a football game that children play. This statement explains how it is designed to keep them safe. It sits alongside our privacy notice and terms of use.

Designed for children by default

  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up to and no profile to fill in.
  • No personal details are ever asked for — no name, email, age or date of birth.
  • No chat and no messaging. Players cannot contact each other.
  • No in-app purchases and no coin balances, so there is nothing to spend

money on inside the game.

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No location access. We never ask for GPS or device location.
  • No profiling, streaks or behavioural tracking.

The leaderboard

The leaderboard is the only shared feature and it is off until the player taps Join leaderboard.

  • A player's display name is a nickname picked from our own word lists, not

anything they type freely.

  • Those lists are screened for offensive, abusive and identifying words,

including blocked word pairs and name-like combinations.

  • A nickname can be changed only once every 7 days, which limits use of the

board as a messaging channel.

  • Report is available on leaderboard entries, with clear reason codes.
  • A report is kept for up to 90 days and is not disclosed to the reported player.
  • Players can leave at any time with Delete my leaderboard data.

Safeguarding position

  • We do not allow content that sexualises, grooms, bullies or harasses anyone.
  • We do not allow sharing of personal contact details, and the nickname system is

designed to make that difficult.

  • Where we become aware of a safeguarding concern involving a child, we will act on

it and will involve the police or the relevant authority where the law or the seriousness of the concern requires it.

  • Parents and carers can contact us at hello@iqappsgroup.co.uk about any concern,

including a nickname or a leaderboard entry.

What parents and carers should know

  • The game can be played entirely offline and without sharing anything.
  • If your child joins the leaderboard, the only things shared are a nickname from

our lists, a score, a chosen country and a random device id created on the phone.

  • There is no way for another player to message your child through the game.
  • You can remove your child's leaderboard entry at any time from Settings.

Not a social network

KICKOLÉ is a single-player game with an optional scoreboard. It is not a social network, it has no user-to-user messaging, and it is not an emergency or support service. If a child is in immediate danger, call 999. For non-urgent safeguarding advice in the UK, the NSPCC helpline is 0808 800 5000.
